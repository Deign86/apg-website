# Storylines — Alpha Premier Realty reel (realty.alphapremiergroup.com)

The one thing: **the new Alpha Premier Realty site is where a business finds its space and starts the deal.**
What the viewer remembers: "Warehouses, offices, shops, homes. Ask Realty and an advisor answers within a day."
Spine: one business owner, one real task (find a space, send the inquiry). The data that travels through it is the inquiry itself: "a 1,200 sqm warehouse in Cavite".

## A. "Every business needs a home" (recommended, built)
Device: a sentence whose last word becomes the picture. Form: object-to-object chain with Apple-style pause and reveal.
The film opens on a real warehouse while the line "Every business needs a…" rolls through *warehouse. office. home.*, then goes black for a moment. The Realty mark appears inside the live site, catches a sweep of light, and the camera pulls back to show the whole site.
- First 2 s: frame 0 already shows the warehouse plate with "Every business needs a warehouse."; on the beat, the word and the photo roll to "office."
- Beats: hook (needs a ___) → reveal (site hero, sweep of light on the gold mark) → proof 1 (the site's real spaces showcase, with real inventory numbers) → proof 2 (Inquire Now → the real form → the inquiry is typed → submit → the site's real "Inquiry sent" toast) → payoff (handshake) → claim ("Where connections grow into success", which becomes the apex site's own headline) → group frame (Realty is enterprise 01) → end card.
- Look: Realty's own black and gold, as Apple films its products on seamless black. Plus Jakarta Sans 800 with tight tracking, white type, gold rationed to the rolling words and the mark.
- Sound: the "Daybreak" bed starts muffled behind glass and opens when the site appears. Effects are a click on each cursor click, key ticks while typing, and one riser into the end-card mark.
- Why it's recommended: a stranger follows it, because everyone knows the need. Every proof shot is the real site doing what it does.

## B. "195 offices. 214 storefronts. One inquiry."
Device: a number lands. Form: type carries the argument, with cuts on the beat.
Giant counts from the October 2026 inventory slam in on the downbeats over the site's photos. Each number earns its place with a shot of the spaces showcase. The inquiry form closes the film as the single action.
- Beats: number → number → number → the form → sent → end card. The look and sound would match A, but harder and faster.
- Not picked: a numbers-only film rests on inventory figures that change weekly and are approximate. It ages fast and says little about why to choose Realty.

## C. "One continuous take down the page"
Device: the site as a place. Form: one continuous take.
The camera never cuts. It flies down the real Realty page (hero → spaces → services → why us → footer), diving into each photo and surfacing in the next section, and ends on the inquiry button.
- Beats: hero → dive → spaces → services → why → the button → end card.
- Not picked: it is the boldest form, but a page fly-through is a site tour, not a story. The banned "series of full-page screenshots" lurks right behind it.
