# alpha-realty/app/components/
## Responsibility
- Contains Alpha Realty's themed sections, inquiry modal, logo, and background components.
- `HomeSection` presents the introduction. `ListingsSection` is the Services page:
  - Header: the admin services (`useServices('realty')`, re-read by `toService`) under the eyebrow and Playfair title with a gold accent word (`services_badge`, `services_heading` rich text, `services_intro`).
  - Cards: rounded cards with a 3:1 photo strip (16:9 on phones; a "Photo coming soon" placeholder when missing or broken) and a glass label (the row's `price` field), number 01–04, title (Plus Jakarta Sans), description, features as gold-dot lines in two columns, "Inquire about this service" and a link to matching listings (`/properties?type=…`, from words in the title).
  - States: loading skeletons and an empty state.
  - Close: the shared call-to-action panel ("Speak with a broker" opens a general inquiry; "Properties").
- `BlogsSection` is the blog (with `ArticleView`, the full-screen reader), and `CareersSection` is the careers page with its application form.
- `InquiryForm` is the inquiry form. `InquireModal` (the popup), `RealtyBackground` (one soft gold light that drifts as you scroll, over the fixed grain and glow layer in the stylesheet; no waves on any page; the drift is a CSS scroll-driven animation and the component only measures the page length in screens on resize) and `AlphaPremierLogo` provide inquiry and shared visual support.
- `reveal.tsx` holds the shared scroll motion of the home and services pages: `Reveal` (staggered reveal in view, none with reduced motion), `rise`, `EASE` and `SectionHeading` (eyebrow plus Playfair title, `page` for an h1).
## Design
- React TypeScript modules use Motion and Tailwind classes for the premium page presentation.
- The parent Realty stylesheet provides black/gold fonts, background, and active-scope rules.
- Modal overlays use `useModalDialog` (Esc, focus trap, `<html>` scroll lock, focus return); the popup starts focus on its heading.
- Glass (`GLASS` in `ui.ts`) is deliberately light: CSS refraction, low quality, no chromatic aberration. SVG refraction made scrolling over photos stutter.
- Text contrast: muted text is at least 55% white (form placeholders 50%, `CAPTION` 55%), which keeps WCAG AA 4.5:1 on the dark surfaces.
- `ui.ts` holds the shared Realty look used by `HomeSection`, `PropertiesSection`, `ListingsSection` and `ListingCard` (including `CTA_PANEL`, the closing call-to-action panel; `FIELD_INPUT`/`FIELD_LABEL`/`FIELD_ERROR` for form fields; `MODAL_OVERLAY`/`MODAL_PANEL`/`MODAL_CLOSE` for popups; `SWIPE_ROW` for phone swipe rows): gold italic accent, eyebrow, focus rings, pill buttons and the quick-liquid glass configs (glass only on controls over photos).
- Props keep navigation and submission feedback controlled by the app shell.
## Flow
- App selects a section from the current tab and mounts it in the animated content region.
- Home links to services/listings, blogs, and generic or property-specific inquiry.
- Listings report the service title to open a prefilled inquiry modal.
- `PropertiesSection` is the live listings page and, given a `hubPath`, a browse page ("hub", `/properties/warehouse-for-lease[/cavite]`): only that hub's listings (`inHub`), the hub's h1/description, a breadcrumb (Available Properties › type + deal hub › hub), no type chips or lease/sale toggle (search still works), its head tags from `hubSeo`, and related hub links (a type + deal hub's indexable areas; an area hub's type + deal hub and the area's other indexable hubs). A path the feed has no hub for shows every listing with a notice, noindex. The grid shows 24 listings, then "Show N more" adds 24 at a time (cards fade up, staggered); any filter or browse-page change starts over at 24. The first 12 cards render with the first paint and the rest right after, in a low-priority transition; the first card's photo loads eagerly. The lease/sale and property-type highlights glide between buttons (motion `layoutId`, instant with reduced motion); type buttons carry an icon and count and are one swipeable row below lg. Unfiltered, the list ends with "Browse by type" (every type + deal hub) and "Popular searches" (indexable area hubs, 24 then "Show all"). An open listing is `ListingView` (`#realty-listing-dialog`), full screen like its own page, over the list, which stays mounted. There is no bar: a floating X (plus Esc and Back) closes it.
  - From lg it is a split screen. The gallery stays in view on the left: the whole photo over a blurred copy, glass previous/next, counter and pause, swipe, arrow keys (not while typing in a field) and thumbnails. It autoplays after 10 s idle, 5 s per photo, for one round back to photo 1, shown by a progress line on the current thumbnail. Hover, focus, the pause button, an open inquiry form, being off screen or reduced motion hold it; a manual change restarts the 10 s.
  - The gallery's photo is the 1200px copy, fetched first (`fetchpriority="high"`, typed in `src/react-fetchpriority.d.ts`), with its 800px card copy (already cached from the list) painted underneath while it loads; thumbnails use the card copies.
  - The right column holds the title with "Listed since" or "Last updated" and the date (`listingDateLabel()`), the price card with "Inquire now" and a short disclaimer (availability, exact location and viewing schedules are coordinated with the admins through Viber; the server-rendered crawler page in `api/properties-page.php` keeps main's longer wording), Type/Area/Size/Ref in one row (`@container`, two rows when narrow), the terms grouped by `groupTerms`, Share and "More <hub h1>" (its indexable area hub, else its type + deal hub). It fits the first screen for nearly every listing.
  - "Inquire now" swaps that column for the inquiry panel: "Back to details", the listing in a gold-framed summary (photo, title, ref, price), then `InquiryForm`. After sending it shows "Inquiry sent" and the ticket. Esc steps back to the details before it closes the listing; focus moves to each panel's heading. On phones the form replaces the photos and details, and the pinned price + Inquire bar hides.
  - "More like this": 3 listings of the same type and deal, same area first; opening one remounts the view (keyed). Opening from the list stores the list scroll in the history state (`Realty.jsx`), so closing, Esc and Back return to the same spot. Hub links are router `<Link>`s to `realtyHref()` paths. Head tags render only once the feed has loaded (react-helmet-async keeps a discarded first render's tags).
- `LiveCount` (in `ListingCard.tsx`) is the quiet pill with the listing count and the last sync ("48 listings · updated 12 min ago"), on the home strip and the Properties header.
- `ListingCard` (also the home "Available now" strip) shows the 800px card copy of the first photo (`ListingPhoto`; lazy except the grid's first card) cropped from the top (Drive photos carry a phone-number strip along the bottom), the deal and Featured tags and "Updated" (`isRecentlyUpdatedListing()`) else "New" (`isNewListing()`), title, area and size, then the price (caption, amount, a reserved note line so prices line up). It also exports `ListingPhoto`, `ListingTags`, `priceCaption` and `realtyHref(path)`: a clean Realty path (`/properties/...`) as is on the Realty subdomain, under `/subsidiaries/realty` elsewhere (cards, hub links and `Realty.jsx` navigation use it, so router state survives on the subdomain).
- `BlogsSection` (blog):
  - Layout: the featured post as a wide card, then category pills with a gliding gold highlight, a count pill and a search pill, and cards (glass category label on the cover, the branded placeholder when a post has no cover or uses the admin's placeholder.svg). "Show N more" adds 6 at a time.
  - Text: admin categories in capitals read as title case, and read time and date show only when the post has them.
  - Loading: placeholder cards while it loads; the built-in sample posts appear only when the blog cannot be loaded.
  - `ArticleView`: a full-screen reader in a portal with a floating X and Esc. Focus starts on the title. It shows the category, date and read time, the title, summary, cover, and the body rendered by the shared `BlogBody` in a reading column, then a call to inquire (closes the article, opens the popup) and 3 related articles (same category first).
- `CareersSection` (careers):
  - Page: an intro with the team photo in a gold offset frame, culture values and benefits as numbered icon cards, the workspace photo collage (a swipe row on phones), and team feedback (a quote carousel with a gliding gold dot; "Share your feedback" posts to `/api/inquire.php` form=feedback, with per-field errors from `useFieldErrors`).
  - Open roles: live search with a status count pill ("3 open roles" / "1 of 3 roles"), role cards with location, type and department pills, "View details" and "Apply now", and the closing "Don't see the right fit?" panel.
  - Job details: a popup sharing the `MODAL_*` styles.
  - Applying: swaps the page for the application form (position select, resume, per-opening extras from `ApplicantExtrasFields`, cover note), posting multipart to `/api/applicants.php` with `form_started_at`; it shows errors inline and an "Application sent" panel with the reference.
- `InquiryForm` posts JSON to `/api/inquire.php`; failures show an inline error instead of success.
  - Fields: name, email and Viber number are required. `company` and `business` (line of business, which landlords ask for) are optional and show in the team email and the admin inbox.
  - For a live listing (a ref) or a service (a title only, from the Services page): no "Inquiry interest" choice; the topic is the property or the service, and one-tap request chips write the message. Listings get viewing, availability, rate and photos requests; services get consultation, proposal, fees and call-back. "Add a note" opens an optional box whose text is added after the requests.
  - After a successful send, the sender's details are kept in this browser (localStorage `apg_realty_contact`, re-validated on read). The next inquiry shows "Sending as …" with "Edit details" and "Not you? Clear", and the consent box comes pre-ticked.
## Integration
- `app/App.tsx` imports components and owns their callback/state wiring.
- Local data contracts and fallback records live in `app/types.ts` and `app/data.ts`.
- `BlogsSection` loads the summary list with `loadBlogList('realty')` (`../../../blogApi.ts`), the list with bodies once on the first search (search also matches article text), and the open post's body with `useBlogBody`; `CareersSection` uses `useCareers` and `/api/applicants.php`.
- Parent `Realty.jsx` manages `alpha-realty-active`, `EnterpriseSeo`, and `EnterpriseNavContext`.
- Unified APG enterprise chrome supplies navigation; there are no local Header/Footer components.
