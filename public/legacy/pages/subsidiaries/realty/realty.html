About Us

Alpha Premier Realty is the real estate division of the Alpha Premier Group, dedicated to delivering smart, strategic, and client-focused property solutions across the Philippines. We specialize in the leasing, sale, and acquisition of high-value assets—from commercial lots and office buildings to logistics hubs, luxury residences, and investment-ready land. With a strong foundation in market knowledge and a commitment to excellence, we help clients navigate the complexities of real estate with confidence and clarity.

Our approach combines nationwide reach with personalized service, ensuring that every transaction is not only seamless but also aligned with your long-term goals. Whether you're an investor, business owner, developer, or homebuyer, we provide the expertise and network to unlock opportunities and maximize potential. At Alpha Premier Realty, we don’t just deal in properties—we build value and lasting partnerships.

Services Offered

Commercial Lots & Office Buildings

At Alpha Premier Realty, we understand that location is a critical factor in business success. That’s why we provide a diverse portfolio of commercial lots and office buildings situated in highly strategic areas. Whether you’re establishing a regional headquarters, launching a flagship retail space, or seeking a future-proof investment, our commercial properties are selected for their accessibility, growth potential, and prestige. We cater to startups, SMEs, and large enterprises alike—offering spaces that elevate your brand and support your operational goals.

Warehouses & Logistics Hubs

We specialize in industrial and logistics real estate solutions designed to meet the demands of supply chain efficiency, e-commerce fulfillment, and large-scale storage. Our warehouses are located in strategic logistics corridors, ensuring connectivity to ports, highways, and transport terminals. Whether you need a compact storage space or a large logistics hub, our facilities are built to scale with your operations—designed for functionality, safety, and long-term efficiency.

High-End Residential Units & Condominiums

Discover refined living in Metro Manila’s top vertical communities or in the quiet elegance of suburban enclaves. Our portfolio of upscale residences is carefully curated for individuals and families seeking more than just a place to live—each home offers an elevated lifestyle, long-term value, and convenience. Whether you're an end-user or investor, we provide move-in ready or pre-selling units developed by the country’s most reputable names in real estate.

Premier Land Investments

For developers, long-term investors, and visionary entrepreneurs, we offer access to valuable landholdings in fast-growing and strategically located areas. Whether your project involves residential subdivisions, commercial developments, tourism ventures, or simply land banking, Alpha Premier Realty ensures each parcel is selected for its potential, documentation readiness, and development viability. We guide you through due diligence, zoning compliance, and investment feasibility to ensure a seamless acquisition process.
