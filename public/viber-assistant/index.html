APG Viber Assistant

An internal tool of Alpha Premier Realty, part of Alpha Premier Group

APG Viber Assistant helps the Alpha Premier Realty admin team answer property inquiries on Viber. It keeps our property listings, which are stored in the company's own Google Drive, in step with the conversations our staff have with clients.

What it does

  • Finds a property listing in the company's Google Drive and sends its client-facing details and photos to a client's Viber chat when a staff member asks for it.
  • Adds new listings to the company Drive: it creates the listing folder, the listing document and the property photos, so every listing our team collects is stored in one place.
  • Updates or reformats listing documents that are already in the company Drive.

Who uses it

Only Alpha Premier Realty staff, on the company's office computer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. The only Google account it connects to is the company's own Google account, which a staff member authorizes once through Google's sign-in.

Google Drive access

The app asks for access to the company's Google Drive because property listings, documents and photos live there and the app must both read them and add new ones. It works only with the listing folders our staff manage, and only to carry out the tasks above. Details are in the APG Viber Assistant privacy policy.
