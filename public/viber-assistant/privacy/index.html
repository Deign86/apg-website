APG Viber Assistant privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how APG Viber Assistant ("the app"), an internal tool operated by Alpha Premier Realty, part of Alpha Premier Group, accesses, uses, stores and protects Google user data. It adds to the Alpha Premier Group privacy policy, which covers the rest of our services. The app's purpose is described on the APG Viber Assistant page.

Whose data the app accesses

The app connects to one Google account only: Alpha Premier Group's own company account. It is used by our staff on the company's office computer and is not offered to the public, so it does not ask members of the public to sign in with Google.

Google data the app accesses

With the company account's permission (the Google Drive scope), the app accesses the company's Google Drive files that hold our property listings:

  • listing folder and file names, and the text of listing documents;
  • property photos and videos stored in those folders;
  • the account's name and email address, to confirm which account is signed in.

How the app uses this data

  • To find a listing and send its client-facing details and photos to a client on Viber when a staff member asks for it. The internal part of a listing (such as owner contact numbers and commission terms) is never sent to clients.
  • To create new listing folders, listing documents and photo files in the company Drive.
  • To update or reformat listing documents in the company Drive.

The app does not use Google data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to build profiles of anyone.

Sharing

Google data is not shared with anyone except: clients who receive the client-facing part of a listing on Viber at a staff member's request; and service providers that run the tool for us, such as the AI assistant provider that carries out staff instructions, which process the data only to perform the requested task. Google data is not used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Storage and protection

  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the company's office computer and is never published or shared.
  • Files the app downloads to send to a client are kept in a local cache and deleted automatically after about three days.
  • Listings themselves remain in the company's Google Drive, under the company's control.

Retention and deletion

The app does not keep its own copy of Drive data beyond the short-lived cache above. The company can revoke the app's access at any time from its Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which stops all further access. To request deletion of any data related to the app, email us at the address below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

APG Viber Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
